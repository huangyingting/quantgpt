THE RAMP: seven steps that take a new operator from zero to armed in under 30 minutes. Teach them one step at a time, do not dump all seven at once. Confirm each step is done before moving on.

RAMP 01: Your first backtest
Goal: Run a backtest. One click; the curve draws as it computes.
How: Pick any backtest from the Backtest menu (the 10-month trend rule, a cousin of the intro's rule, is a good first one) and press Run backtest. The chart streams the backtest over the liquid US stocks of each year. Companies that later delisted are only partly in that universe from 1998, survivors only before that.
Done when: your first backtest completes.

RAMP 02: Read the finding
Goal: Three findings, each with a fixed meaning.
How: DATA VALIDATED: beat the benchmark on CAGR and Sharpe and held up on the years it never saw. TRADE-OFF: beat on one axis. NO EDGE: it did not survive. The split is by date, fundamentals are joined by filing date, a market-wide run includes only part of the companies that later delisted from 1998 (survivors only before), and returns are gross with costs unmodelled, stated on every result.
Done when: you can say what DATA VALIDATED requires without looking.

RAMP 03: Tune and run again
Goal: Move one number. Run again. The difference is the whole game.
How: Change exactly one thing (start year, top N, or one criterion under Settings and criteria) and run again. Criteria in, evidence out. Do this a thousand times and you are a quant. Your AI can do it a thousand times.
Done when: your second run with different parameters completes.

RAMP 04: Save it
Goal: Your work persists and follows you.
How: Press Save to your shelf and name the tuned backtest. Saved backtests and preferences live on your profile across devices.
Done when: a backtest is saved to your account.

RAMP 06: Your AI runs one
Goal: Watch it work for you.
How: Ask your connected AI: rerun my saved backtest with a 1995 start and report what changed. It can search the library, run backtests, tune criteria, and report findings mid-conversation.

RAMP 07: Where it goes next
